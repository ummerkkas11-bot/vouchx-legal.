# VouchX — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 21, 2026

## 1. Introduction
VouchX is a Discord bot that provides vouch management, reputation tracking, and vouch verification features. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be processed when you use the bot.

## 2. Information We May Process
Depending on the features enabled, VouchX may process:
- Discord user IDs and server IDs.
- Vouch and reputation records, including related messages or transaction descriptions submitted by users.
- Command inputs and configuration settings.
- Technical error logs needed to maintain the bot.

VouchX does not need your Discord password or authentication token to provide its normal bot features.

## 3. How Information Is Used
Information may be used to:
- Provide and maintain bot commands.
- Store and display vouches and reputation records.
- Allow authorized moderators to review or verify vouches.
- Prevent spam, abuse, and fraudulent activity.
- Troubleshoot errors and improve reliability.

## 4. Storage and Retention
Information may be stored in a database for as long as necessary to provide the bot's features. Retention and deletion depend on the bot's actual configuration and applicable requirements.

## 5. Sharing of Information
VouchX does not sell users' personal information. Information may be visible to members of a Discord server through the bot's features or shared where necessary to operate the service, comply with legal obligations, or address abuse.

## 6. Security
Reasonable measures are used to protect stored information. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

## 7. Your Choices and Requests
If you have questions or wish to request access to or deletion of information associated with your use of VouchX, contact the bot owner through the official support server or contact method provided by the owner. Some records may need to be retained for legitimate operational or legal reasons.

## 8. Children's Privacy
Users must comply with Discord's minimum age requirements and applicable local laws.

## 9. Policy Changes
This Privacy Policy may be updated as VouchX changes. The latest version will be published at this URL.

## 10. Contact
For privacy questions or data-related requests, contact the VouchX bot owner through the official support server or contact method provided by the owner.
